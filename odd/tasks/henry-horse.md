Objective: the whole horse (mesh, rig and clips) becomes the CC-BY "Horse" by henrysteve973 (henry_7), keeping every system that uses the horse working: coats, tack and reins, rider, draught harness, hoof dust, jumps.
Branch: `feat/henry-horse` (from 2d4809a). User commits. Delivery: ask-on-risk (forecast well over 400 lines). Tests: `cargo test -p pampa-render` / `-p pampa-core` / `-p game` capped (MemoryMax=5G, `--test-threads=2`); `python -m unittest` for `tools/`.
