# Pillion: a second rider "en ancas"

Objective: a second person rides on the croup behind the rider of a saddled horse (Bairoletto behind Lisandro in Act 6).
Branch: `feat/pillion`. Delivery: the user commits (no agent commits). Tests: `cargo test -p pampa-render` (systemd-run memory cap, `--test-threads=2`) and `cargo test -p game`.

## Specs

- **S1** (todo.md, Act 6): "la huida a caballo con él en ancas"; "Necesita [...] alguien que va en ancas". A saddled horse with a rider can carry a second person seated on the croup, behind the saddle, facing forward, following the horse in every gait.
  - Assumption: the pillion has no reins and no stirrups; the legs hang astride along the flanks, the knees slightly bent.
  - User (L6, 2026-11-25): "si, debería agarrargse del jinete o de alguna "manija" o algo en la montura (creq que ne era común agarres en las monturas en aquella época)". The pillion holds on to the front rider (both hands at the rider's waist); the recado criollo has no handle.
  - User (L6): "bairolétto tiene los mobros muey atrás, por detrás de la espalda no se fisionomicamente correcto". The shoulders sit in their natural place over the trunk, not behind the back.
  - User (L6): "bairoletto está sentado muy atrás, es una zona muy inestable del caballo donde se mueven mucho las caderas del caballo". The pillion sits right behind the recado, close to the rider, not over the hips.
  - Acceptance: the pillion's model hangs from the horse, sits on the croup skin (not floating, not sunk), its body clears the horse, the recado and the front rider; it disappears when the pillion is removed or the horse visual changes.
- **S2** (dev console): a debug command seats Bairoletto behind the player while riding, and removes him again, to see S1 in the game. Help text and console docs list it. Done as `pillion on` / `pillion off` (on foot: "pillion: get on a horse first"); he stays on until `pillion off`, also across dismounting and remounting.
- **S3**: the user checks it in the game (`pillion on` while mounted, walk, trot, gallop, jump) and approves the look or asks for corrections.

Out of scope here: Act 6 itself (the escape, the shot, the posse), mounting/dismounting animations of the pillion.
