# Carts and sulkies

Objective: horse-drawn sulkies and carts (carretas, carretones) parked in settlements and travelling the roads between them, at maximum realism. Branch: feat/pampa-fauna (shared with uncommitted fauna work; the user owns commits). Delivery: ask-on-risk. Tests: cargo test (MemoryMax=5G, --test-threads=2), python unittest for tools/.

## Specs

- S1. Models: own Blender-scripted period sulky, carreta and carretón (with their draught animals: horse for the sulky, horses or oxen as fits the period for the carts), at the quality floor of the rhea v8 / felids ("máximo realismo"). Free CC0/CC-BY third-party models or textures are allowed but each download needs the user's permission first, and is credited in CREDITS.md and next to the asset.
- S2. Placement in settlements (user): parked vehicles are horse-drawn carretones and sulkies only, never an ox carreta (period distribution, L6). Counts: "uno o dos en ciudades, uno en pueblos, uno muy esporádicamente en aldeas". Earlier todo.md wording: "agrgear sulkys y carretas en ciudades y pueblos uno o dos en pueblos, uno solo en aldeas y unas pares en ciudades, en General Pico un Ford T en la intendencia". The latest request rules on counts; the Ford T at the intendencia of General Pico stays as part of the original item (assumption: include it).
- S3. Travellers on the roads (user; period distribution L6: carretones carry the freight, sulkies the people, and the ox carreta is rare, an occasional traveller on a rural road): "algunos en caminos entre poblado y poblado, es decir viajeros que van de alguna estancia a alguna pulpería, de algun almacen de ramos generales a la pulpería del otro pueblo cosas así, que tenga vida el mapa". Each traveller has an origin and a destination among existing places (estancias, pulperías, general stores) and drives along the road network between them.
- S4. "buscando siempre el máximo realismo": period-correct shapes, materials, wear, and movement: wheels roll with ground speed, draught animals walk or trot in step, the vehicle follows the road.
