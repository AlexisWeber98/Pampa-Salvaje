## Specs

- S1. User (2026-11-03, verbatim): "actualicemos las palomas tambien". Species, chosen by the coordinator acting for the user (assumption): eared dove (torcaza, *Zenaida auriculata*) for the big flocks, and picazuro pigeon (picazuró, *Patagioenas picazuro*). Both are common in La Pampa.
- S3. User (2026-11-03, verbatim): "palomas y gorrienes argentinos". Sparrows, chosen by the coordinator (assumption): the house sparrow (gorrión, *Passer domesticus*; introduced to Argentina around 1872 and common in towns by 1919) and the rufous-collared sparrow (chingolo, *Zonotrichia capensis*), the native "Argentine sparrow" of the countryside. Unlike the pigeons they live low and on the ground, so phase 2 needs new behaviour: town streets and roofs for the gorrión, field edges and fences for the chingolo.
- S2. Phase 1: own Blender models, clips and preview sheets beside reference photos, at the raptors' realism (real pixels where licences allow), for approval. Phase 2: wire them into the sky flocks.
