# Feature: building-realism

Objective: maximum realism on every building in cities, towns and villages, plus the missing rural dwellings. Branch `feat/pampa-raptors` (shared with the raptor writer on disjoint files; the user owns commits). Tests: `cargo test` capped with `systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=5G` and `--test-threads=2`; python unittest for tools/.

## Specs

- S1. todo.md (user, verbatim): "máximo realismo posible en todas las construcciones de pueblos, ciudades y aldeas (bancos, intendencia y demás edificios: texturas propias, desgaste, zócalos manchados, detalle". Every existing building type gets its own textures, weathering, stained plinths and architectural detail, at the 1919 La Pampa period.
- S2. todo.md (user, verbatim): "(faltan ranchos de madera, casillas, y ranchos de barro y paja))". Add wooden ranchos, casillas, and mud-and-straw ranchos, placed where they belong (assumption: villages, outskirts and the countryside, following the existing settlement placement).
- S3. todo.md standing instruction (user, verbatim): "descargar modelos renderizados de terceros de libre uso siempre que sea posible, con el mayor detalle posible mientras sea soportado por el hardware actual, de no ser posible utilartexturas (descargables o ya disponibles)". Free CC0/CC-BY assets only, credited in CREDITS.md and where used; never Sketchfab.

- L7 (2026-11-03, writer) T1, what shipped:
  - Weathering: `assets/shaders/building_weathering.wesl`, an `ExtendedMaterial<StandardMaterial, Weathering>` (`facade::materials::BuildingMaterial`, built from each surface's standard material by `setup_weathering`). A building mesh carries a second UV: the height above the ground under the building, and its `Weather` packed as wear (hundredths, integer part) plus damp (fraction, halved on the `+Z` street face). Per-surface uniforms: `Surface::weathering` (splash-back, rising damp, dark foot, ledge dust) and `Surface::breakup` (macro tone, fresh-mud patches, lumps). Rising damp has an irregular, domain-warped tide line, a broken brown tide mark and a thin crusty salt band, and is stronger on shaded south-facing walls. Only meshes with that UV use the material (`FacadeMaterials::insert`, `materials::weathers`).
  - Shape decals (`facade/grime.rs`, `Surface::Grime`, vertex RGBA via `Shape::faded`): rain streaks under sills and cornice spans (never over openings), rust runs on casilla iron, and soil at the foot of every wall (0.35 m, 7.5 cm over the sidewalk, 2.5 cm elsewhere). Their alpha scales with wear. Only a share of the projections streaks (`Shape::set_streaks`, `town_buildings::streaks`): none on the bank and the Intendencia.
  - Wear by type (`town_buildings::wear`): bank/Intendencia 0.12–0.22, church/comisaría 0.3–0.45, station 0.4–0.55, almacén 0.35–0.55, houses 0.2–0.7, pulpería 0.7–0.95, outskirts 0.6–1.0; tanks and windmills none.
  - Own textures per type (Poly Haven, CC0, 1K, KTX2 via `tools/textures_to_ktx2.sh`): bank `concrete_wall_004` (Ashlar), Intendencia and church `plastered_wall_02` (Render ochre / lime white), comisaría `painted_worn_brick` (LimeBrick), pulpería and poor outskirts `preconcrete_wall_001` (OldLime), station `red_brick_03` (StationBrick), stained cement plinths `concrete_wall_008` (Plinth), persianas `green_rough_planks` (Shutter).
