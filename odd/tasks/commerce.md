# Commerce: buying from the pulpero and the almacenero
Objective: talk to the pulpero and the almacenero at their counters and buy from them with the HUD's money, the goods going to the inventory; a new Trade ability, unlocked by `unlock all` in development; a leather style for the shop list and the inventory.
Branch: `feat/commerce` (from 2ce0096). User commits. Delivery: ask-on-risk. Tests: `cargo test -p pampa-core` / `-p pampa-render` / `-p game` capped (MemoryMax=5G, `--test-threads=2`).

## Specs
- **S1** (todo.md #3, user): "hablar con el pulpero y el almacenero y comprarles: acercarse al mostrador, que atiendan, y comprar víveres, herramientas, armas, etc. (en el almacén, y bebida en la pulpería), pagando con la plata del HUD y guardando lo comprado en el inventario (tecla I)."
- **S2** (user, 2026-11-12): "para la mision que lo ocupa, sin embargo, el "truco" Unlock All debe desbloquearlo para poder probarse en desarrolĺo". A new ability Trade (Comercio): the story unlocks it later (Evaristo's tutorial, Act 8); `unlock all` unlocks it now. Without it the keeper serves but does not sell: "Volvé cuando tengas con qué".
- **S3** (catalog and prices, proposed and accepted by the user, 2026-11-12: "si"). Pesos of 1919.
  - Pulpería: vaso de caña $ 0,10, vaso de ginebra $ 0,15, vaso de vino $ 0,10, botella de caña $ 0,80, tabaco y papel de armar $ 0,25, fósforos $ 0,05, galleta $ 0,10.
  - Almacén: yerba 1 kg $ 0,80, azúcar 1 kg $ 0,40, charqui 1 kg $ 0,60, harina 1 kg $ 0,20, velas $ 0,05; cuchillo $ 3, hacha $ 4, pala $ 3, maneador $ 2,50; lazo $ 4; alpargatas $ 1,20, poncho $ 8; armas (need the Weapons ability): revólver $ 25, Winchester $ 60, caja de 25 balas $ 2,50.
- **S4** (how to buy, accepted): at the counter "F: hablar con el pulpero" (or "con el almacenero"); a greeting subtitle and a list with the player's money on top; W/S to choose, F to buy one, Esc to leave; what the player cannot afford is dimmed; bought goods go to the inventory (I) with quantities.
- **S5** (user, 2026-11-12): "igual el estilo del inventario es temporal hay que mejorarlo, me gusnstaría un estilo de fondo de cuero". The shop list and the inventory get a leather background. Assumption: a CC0 leather texture (download needs the user's permission).
