# Menu assets

Shown and played by the title screen, the pause menu and the Sonido screen (`game/src/menu.rs`). `tools/menu_poster.sh` bakes `poster.png`, `band.png` and `vignette.png` from `sources/`. The band and the vignette are made from scratch.

| File | Author / source | Licence | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|

| `sources/bairoletto_1928_restored.jpg` | Winifreda police photograph of Juan Bautista Bairoletto (1928), restored by Roly Santos (2021); uploaded by Laromanacoop. Wikimedia Commons, `File:Juan_Bautista_Bairoletto.jpg` | CC BY-SA 4.0 | Downscaled to 1200 px wide |
| `sources/mate_cosido.png` | Argentine police photographs of Mate Cosido (born Segundo David Peralta), profile and front, 1930s; author unknown. Copied from Infobae, "La increíble vida de Mate Cosido" (2023-07-28), which credits no source. | Public domain in Argentina (Ley 11.723 art. 34: photographs, 25 years from publication); assessed by the project | Converted from WebP to PNG |
