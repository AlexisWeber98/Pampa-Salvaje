# Credits: inventory item icons

Every icon here is a 256 px PNG rendered in Blender (Cycles) by `tools/item_icons.py`. Five come from Poly Haven models; the other twenty (the drinks, food, sacks, rawhide, clothing, arms, the letter and the coins) are modelled procedurally in that script, our own work (CC0), some of them dressed with Poly Haven textures. The models and textures are not kept in the repository, only the icons.

Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) publishes every asset under the **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal** public domain dedication (https://polyhaven.com/license). The licence doesn't require credit, but we give it anyway. The project owner downloaded them to `~/Downloads/polyhaven/` on 2026-11-12.
